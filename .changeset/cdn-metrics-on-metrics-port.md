With `cdn` set, the app's Prometheus endpoint on port `9464` now serves the CDN counters: `knext_cdn_edge_decision_total` (labelled `result` and `reason`, so you can see why a page is not edge-cached), `knext_cdn_foreign_edge_strip_total` and `knext_cdn_purge_total` (labelled `provider` and `result`). They reach the port through the local metrics listener your app's `src/instrumentation.ts` starts; apps generated by `knext create` already start it, and the CDN docs show the lines to add to an older app. Without `cdn` nothing changes, and the `vinext` build target, which has no CDN support, serves no CDN series.
